📋 Clinic Rota Notifications

Get your room rota sent to your phone every morning

On your clinic days, you'll automatically receive a message at 7am with a direct link to that day's room rota. One tap and it opens. Nothing else to remember.
🔒 Your information is safe. This service only stores your name, working days, and a notification ID. It never accesses patient data or any other files. It was set up by Dr Nivern for clinic staff only.
1
Install the Telegram messaging app

Telegram is a free, secure messaging app — it's how your rota gets sent to your phone. If you already have it, skip to step 2.

It is not a scam. It does not access your contacts, photos, or any personal data. Many NHS staff use it.

📱 iPhone — Download from App Store 📱 Android — Download from Google Play
Set up Telegram with your mobile number. It takes about 2 minutes. You can delete it later — your rota notifications will still have been set up.
2
Find your personal notification number

You need a unique number so the system knows where to send your messages. Here's how to get it:

Tap the button below — it opens a tool called @userinfobot in Telegram. Send it any message (e.g. "hi") and it will instantly reply with your number.

Open @userinfobot in Telegram
⚠️ Important — write this number down before moving on The reply will contain a line starting with Id: followed by your personal number — for example: Id: 8738780010

Write that number down now on a piece of paper. You will need it in step 4.

To copy it on your phone: press and hold on the number, then tap Copy.
3
Register with the rota service

Tap the button below to open the rota service in Telegram, then tap Start. This lets the service send messages to your phone.

Open the Rota Notification Service
Once it opens, tap Start at the bottom. You may see a message — you can ignore it and move on to step 4.
4
Sign up — takes 1 minute

Fill in the short form below. You will need:

✅ Your name
✅ Which days you work
✅ The number you wrote down in step 2

Open the Sign-Up Form
You only need to do this once. If your working days change, fill in the form again or speak to Dr Nivern.
✅

That's everything. You'll receive your first rota message at 7am on your next clinic day.

If something doesn't work, contact Dr Nivern.

Common questions

Is this safe? Will it steal my information? No. This service was set up by Dr Nivern specifically for clinic staff. It only stores your name, working days, and notification number. It never accesses patient data, your contacts, photos, or anything else on your phone.
Do I have to keep Telegram installed? Yes — Telegram is how your rota message is delivered to your phone. You can keep it in a folder if you prefer. It takes up very little space.
What if I miss the 7am message? The link stays in your Telegram messages all day. You can also send any message to the rota service at any time and it will reply with today's rota instantly.
The link opened but asked me to sign in Use your normal NHS Microsoft email and password — the same one you use for Microsoft Teams at work. If you're already logged into the Teams app on your phone, it may open automatically.
What if my working days change? Fill in the sign-up form again with your updated days and let Dr Nivern know.
I can't find the number in step 2 When @userinfobot replies, look for the line that starts with Id: — it will be followed by a string of numbers, for example Id: 8738780010. That whole number after the colon is what you need.
