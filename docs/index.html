📋 Clinic Rota Notifications

Get your room rota sent to your phone every morning

On your clinic days, you'll automatically receive a message at 7am with a direct link to that day's room rota. One tap and it opens. Nothing to remember.
1
Install Telegram

Telegram is a free messaging app — this is how the rota link gets sent to you. If you already have it, skip to step 2.

📱 Download on iPhone (App Store) 📱 Download on Android (Google Play)
Set up Telegram with your personal mobile number. It takes about 2 minutes.
2
Find your Telegram ID

You need a personal ID number so the bot knows where to send your messages. Here's how to get it:

Tap the button below — it opens a bot called @userinfobot in Telegram. Send it any message (e.g. "hi") and it will instantly reply with your ID number.

Open @userinfobot in Telegram
Look for the line that says Id: 123456789 — write down or screenshot that number. You'll need it in step 4.
3
Start the rota bot

You need to start a conversation with the rota bot once so it's allowed to message you.

Open @SLPRotaSlavebot in Telegram

Once it opens, tap Start. That's it — you don't need to send anything.

4
Sign up — takes 1 minute

Fill in the form below with your name, which days you work, and the ID number you got in step 2.

Open Signup Form
You only need to do this once. If your working days change, fill in the form again and let the practice manager know.
✅

That's everything. You'll receive your first rota message at 7am on your next clinic day. If something doesn't work, speak to my Master.

Common questions

Is this secure? The bot only sends you a link to the room rota — the same document you'd open manually in Teams. It doesn't contain any patient information and never accesses anything else.
What if I miss the message? The link stays in your Telegram chat and works all day. You can tap it any time, talk to it, it will respond.
What if my rota days change? Fill in the signup form again with your updated days and let the practice manager know so they can update your record.
Do I have to use Telegram? For now, yes — it's the simplest way to get a reliable notification with a clickable link. Most people find it quick to set up.
